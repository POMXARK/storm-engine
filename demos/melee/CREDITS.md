# Credits

The melee demo's binary assets are committed in `resource/` and `assets/` and rebuilt by `run.sh` from free sources: `../dialog/fetch_assets.sh` downloads the font, `make_melee.py` generates everything else — the yard (from the location demo's generator), the two fencers and their fight actions (`../common/sailor.py`, `../common/sailor_fight.py`), the saber, its textures and the enemy bars.

| Asset | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Font: PT Serif Regular | ParaType | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/OFL.txt`) | [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptserif) |
| Yard, fencers, animations, saber, bars | generated by the scripts of this repository | same as the repository | `make_melee.py` |
