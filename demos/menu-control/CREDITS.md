Credits
=======

The same assets the interface demo carries, generated here from its downloaded files: the
"OldStandard" font by Fyodor (Old Stylee), `assets/OFL.txt` of this demo is its licence and
`assets/OFL.meta` names its source page; `ninth_wave.jpg` by Arvids
(https://deviantart.com/greatarendeguy, "Ninth wave reworked") stands behind the menu in
`resource/textures/interfaces/menu_back.tga.tx` together with the ship picture of the game
in `menu_icons.tga.tx`, its own licence lives next to the interface demo that draws it.

The menu of this demo is the item list of the interface demo, `mainmenu.ini` copied verbatim
from it; the autotest adds no art of its own.
