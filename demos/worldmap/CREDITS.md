# Credits

The world map demo's binary assets are committed in `resource/` and `assets/` and rebuilt by `run.sh` from free sources: `../dialog/fetch_assets.sh` downloads the font, and `make_worldmap.py` generates everything else — the four islands with their textures and areas, the base model with the labels, the ships, the compass rose, the sea and its ripples, the clouds, the rain and the lightning, and the interface of the clock, the wind and the morale.
